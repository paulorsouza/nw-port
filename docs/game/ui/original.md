# Screens and HUD of the original game

Survey of the game files and what the port implements. Detailed survey of each canvas (elements, texts, textures,
Lua) in `extract/ui/survey.md`, generated by `python3 tools/ui_survey.py`. Work cycle and LyShine rules:
`docs/game/ui/README.md`.

The 164 LyShine screens are in `extract/ui/lyshineui/**/*.dynamicuicanvas.json` (`tools/linux/build_all.sh` step 03).
The game builds almost everything in Lua (buttons, lists, options): the canvas holds only the frame and the templates.
The texts of buttons and options are in the `.luac` files (`strings` shows the `@ui_*` keys, translated in
`extract/loc`).

## Camera (`libs/camera/gamecamera.xml`)

171 camera states. The ones that matter for moving and fighting (CryEngine: meters, vertical FOV):

| State | Offset (right, forward, up) | Distance | Zoom | FOV | Pitch (down / up) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DynamicCamera` (default) | 0.5 / 0 / 1.7 | 2.0 | 0.2 to 6.0 | 50 | −70° / 60° |
| `NormalCamera` | 0.5 / 0 / 1.7 | 2.7 | 0.2 to 6.0 | 50 | −70° / 30° |
| `MidCameraSprint` | 0.7 / 0 / 1.7 | 4.0 | — | 50 | — |
| `CloseCamera` | 0.5 / 0 / 1.6 | 1.7 | fixed | 50 | — |
| `LockedCamera` (target locked) | 0.4 / 0 / 1.8 | — | — | 50 | −35° / 35° |
| `UI_Inventory` | 0.77 / 0 / 0.95 | 2.75 | fixed | 50 (ignores the player's option) | −70° / −10°; `BlendLookDirection`: the character turns to face the camera |

Transitions between states: 0.2 s (`SmoothTime`). FOV 50 is vertical: at 16:9 it gives ~79° horizontal (Unreal uses
horizontal). Maximum view distance 3000 m.

## Esc menu (`escapemenu`)

840 × 800 window in the center (`EscapeMenuHoder`), sections with the game's titles: `@ui_escapemenu_game` (Game),
`@ui_escapemenu_support` (Support), `@ui_escapemenu_leave_game` (Leave Game), and the shop on the right. Buttons from
`escapemenu.luac`: `@ui_navmenu_settings` (Settings), `@ui_escapemenu_unstuck`, `@ui_escapemenu_help`,
`@ui_escapemenu_exit_to_main_menu`, `@ui_escapemenu_exit_to_desktop`, among others.

## Options (`options`)

Tabs: `@ui_options_visuals` (Video), `@ui_options_audio` (Audio), `@ui_options_gameplay` (Gameplay),
`@ui_options_kbm_controls` (Key bindings), `@ui_options_controller`. 587 keys in `options.luac`. The ones the port
uses:

- Video: `@ui_graphics_settings` (Video Quality: Low/Medium/High/Very High), `@ui_window_mode`, `@ui_resolution`,
  `@ui_max_fps` (30…360/Unlimited), `@ui_enable_vsync`, `@ui_options_fov` (Field of View), `@ui_enable_motionBlur`,
  `@ui_show_fps`, `@ui_options_brightness`.
- Audio: `@ui_mastervolume`, `@ui_musicvolume`, `@ui_sfxvolume`, `@ui_ambVolume`, `@ui_ambientmusicvolume`,
  `@ui_uivolume`.
- Gameplay: `@ui_language` (Text Language), `@ui_looksensitivityhorizontal`, `@ui_looksensitivityvertical`,
  `@ui_invertlook`, `@ui_cameraShake`, `@ui_options_damage_numbers_display_mode`.

## World map (`lyshineui/worldmap/magicmap`, M key)

- Full screen. The content is tiles `lyshineui/worldtiles/newworld_vitaeeterna/map_l<z>_y<yyy>_x<xxx>.dds` of
  1024 px. Level 1: 56 × 56 tiles of 256 m (4 px/m; the world is 14,336 m). Level z: 256·2^(z−1) m per tile, indices
  in steps of 2^(z−1); levels 1 to 8. The y index grows to the north (checked by overlaying the gatherables: only
  this way do they avoid the sea).
- Windsward (r_+04_+01) = x 8192–10240, y 2048–4096. The town (`Settlement_SouthWindsward_Complete`) is at
  (9349.6, 2702.2) with a rotation of −94°.
- Parts: gradient `map/map_gradient` (top/bottom) and `map/map_gradientside` (sides), scale bar `map/scale_key` with
  the distance text below it, buttons on the right (`CenterPlayer`, `ZoomInButton`, `ZoomOutButton`: `buttonbg` +
  `heroframedisabled2023`), player icon `map/icon/player`. POI icons in `map/icon/pois` (194), gatherables in
  `map/icon/gatherables` and NPCs in `map/icon/npcs`.
- POIs: position in `slices/pois/territories/r_<x>_<y>_pois.dynamicslice` (entities with
  `TerritoryDataProviderComponent` "territory id" + `TransformComponent`, translation in the last 3 values); name
  (`NameLocalizationKey`) and icon (`MapIcon`, .png in the data = .dds in the pak) in
  `pointofinterestdefinitions/javelindata_poidefinitions_<x>_<y>`. Windsward: 78 territories, 66 with an icon.

## Key bindings (`libs/config/defaultprofile.xml`)

The game's action map (CryEngine actionmaps), with the default keys per action: `moveforward/back/left/right`
W/S/A/D, `autorun` = (equals key), `walk` left Ctrl (hold), `jump` Space, `dodge` left Shift, `attack_primary` left
mouse button, `block` right mouse button, `ability1–3` Q/R/F, `quickslot-weapon1/2` 1/2,
`quickslot-consumable-1..4` 3–6, `sheathe` ~, `crouch_toggle` C, `mount_summon` X, `toggleInventoryWindow` Tab (in
`includes/uishared.xml`). The on-screen names come from `ui_movefoward`, `ui_runtoggle`, `ui_walk`, `ui_rolldodge`,
`ui_attack`, `ui_block`, `ui_ability1..3`, `ui_weapon1/2`, `ui_consumable1`, `ui_interact`, `ui_inventory`. Conflict
in the game: `ui_remapPopup_rebindConflict` (the other action is left without a key). In the port the two actions swap
keys.

## Inventory, bags, coins

Full spec of the inventory, equipment and storage screens: `docs/game/ui/inventory.md`.

- `newinventory`: window right of center; items in 122 × 60 tiles (`ItemDraggable/ItemLayout`): background
  `itembglarge<rarity>` + strip `itemraritybglarge<rarity>` + icon, tier in Roman numerals, gear score, quantity over
  `itemnumberbg`. Weight bar (`WeightBar`) and coins in the footer (`CurrencyText`).
- `equipmentv3`: equipment on the left with tabs `tabweaponsarmor` and `tabtools`; slots `framesquare` +
  `equipmentbg` with the type icon when empty.
- `hud2024/actionbar/encumbrance`: encumbrance warning on the HUD.
- Item weight: `Weight` in the itemdefinitions, in tenths of a kg; base limit 1000.
- Bags: items `BagAT1`–`BagAT5`, `BagBT2`–`BagBT5`, `BagAT52` (`ItemClass` Bag, ItemType Armor, in
  `itemdefinitions_master_crafting`); capacity in `javelindata_itemdefinitions_armor` (`Encumbrance`, in tenths:
  AT1 +50, AT2 +100, AT3 +150, AT4 +225, AT5 +300; BT2 +125 … ) and `EncumbrancePerGS` (2–6). They go in their own
  inventory slots. In the port: 3 bag slots in the Tools tab; each equipped bag adds its `Encumbrance` to the max
  weight (`EncumbrancePerGS` left out, no source for the formula). No recipe yet: bags come from F10 > Test items.

## Status

- `hud2024/paperdollstatus`: equipment state (durability) on the HUD.
- `hud2024/xp`: 334 × 58 bar at the bottom center: level (`LevelText`, 27) in three `xpmeternumberframe*` frames,
  `xpmeterfill` bar with a mask and the `afflictionmeteredge` tip, rested XP (`MeterRestedFill`, purple), the
  "XP x N" multiplier and the "+N XP" notice (`DeltaXpText`).
- `statuseffectsv2`, `afflictions2024`: active effects.

## Level, XP and attributes (game tables)

- `javelindata_xpamountsbylevel`: 70 rows (`Level Number` 0–69). `XPToLevel` is the **total** accumulated XP
  (150, 480, 1080…; 2.7 M on the last row). `AttributePoints`: 5 per level at the start, 10 at the end; 510 in total.
  Also `AttributeRespecCost` (coins to redistribute) and `Health` (health per level). How the port reads it: level n
  starts at `XPToLevel[n-1]`; on reaching level n+1 the player gains `AttributePoints[n]`.
- `javelindata_attribute<força|destreza|inteligência|foco|constituição>` (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Focus,
  Constitution): one row per attribute value (5 to 500). `ModifierValue` drops from 1.25 to 0.8 (diminishing returns)
  and `ModifierValueSum` is the running total, used in weapon damage scaling. Focus has `HealScaling`. Constitution
  has `Health`, already accumulated (6 = 40, 14 = 360, 54 = 1908, 500 = 13769). Each attribute starts at 5.
- `javelindata_gameevents`: rewards per event. Gathering: `Gather_<Mining|Logging|Harvesting|Skinning>T<n>` with
  `UniversalExpAmount` (3.35 to 23.45) and trade skill XP (`CategoricalProgressionReward`). Kill: `KillEvent` with
  `UniversalExpAmount` 1. Each gatherable points to its event in the `GameEventID` column of
  `javelindata_gatherables` (only in 454 of the 1779 rows, so it does not show up in the table's first row). The
  tables do not hold the scale of `UniversalExpAmount` (on a kill it is 1, so it is multiplied by something from the
  creature's level).
- Gathering trade skills (`javelindata_categoricalprogression`: Mining, Logging, Harvesting, Skinning, maximum level
  200, 250 with the expansion): curve in `javelindata_tradeskill<tradeskill>` (`InfluenceCost` = XP from the previous
  level to this one; Mining 250 = 678,254 XP), `GatheringEfficiency` (+0.05 per level at the start, 2.2 at 250) and
  `RollBonus` (+10 per level on the loot roll). XP per gather = the event's `CategoricalProgressionReward` (e.g. small
  boulder 64, large pine 199). `RequiredTradeskillLevel` requires a level (large pine 50, the pt-BR "planta da morte" 30).

All extracted in `extract/ui/lyshineui/<folder>` (run `tools/ui_canvas.py` to see the tree). Each one depends on a
system the port does not have:

| Screen | Canvas | Depends on |
|---|---|---|

| Social / Company | `socialpane/*`, `guildmenu/guild` | friends and companies in nw-core |
| Journal and objectives | `objectives/journalscreen`, `objectiveshud` | quests (data `javelindata_objectives*`) |
| Active effects | `statuseffects/statuseffectsv2`, `hud2024/afflictions` | status effects (potions with duration, poisons) |
| Housing | `housing/*` | houses and decoration |
| Territory, projects | `territory/*` | territory ownership, taxes |
| Inn | `inn/inninteractmenu` | respawn/fast travel points |
| Mission board | `missionboard/missionboard` | town missions |
| Emotes | `emoteui/emoteui` | emote animations (there are clips in the pak) |
| Dungeons | `dungeons/dungeonenterscreen` | expeditions |
